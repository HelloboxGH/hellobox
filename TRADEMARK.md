# HelloBox trademark policy

The HelloBox source code is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). That license covers
the code. It does not give you any rights to the name "HelloBox" or to the HelloBox logo.
Those are ours, and this page says how you can use them.

In this policy, "HelloBox marks" means the name HelloBox (in any capitalization or spacing),
the HelloBox logo and icon, and any name or logo that looks confusingly similar.

## What you can do without asking

- **Run an unmodified copy for yourself.** If you self-host HelloBox, built from our
  releases without changes, for your own team or company, you can keep the HelloBox name
  and logo in the app.
- **Say what your project is based on.** "Based on HelloBox", "a fork of HelloBox" and
  "compatible with HelloBox" are fine in a README, a description or a blog post, as long
  as it is clear your project is not the official one.
- **Write about HelloBox.** Reviews, tutorials, comparisons, talks and news can use the
  name to refer to the project. Use it as plain text, and do not suggest we endorse you.
- **Link to us.** Linking to the repository or to hellobox.app is always fine.

## What you need another name for

- **Forks and modified versions.** If you change the code and distribute it, give your
  version its own name and its own logo, and remove the HelloBox marks from the app, the
  package names, the domain and the repository.
- **Hosted services.** If you run HelloBox as a service for other people (free or paid),
  that service needs its own name and logo, even if you run our code unmodified. The
  HelloBox name belongs to the official hosted service, HelloBox Cloud.
- **Anything that looks official.** Don't use the HelloBox marks in a company name, domain
  name, social media handle, app store listing, product name or package name, and don't
  use a name or logo close enough to ours that people could think your product comes from
  us or is endorsed by us.

## Other things to know

- Don't change our logo or its colors and then use it as your own.
- If you are unsure, ask us before you publish. We are happy to say yes to reasonable uses.
- We can update this policy. A use that was fine earlier may need to change later.
- Third-party names and logos shown in HelloBox (email providers, sign-in services and
  other products HelloBox connects to) belong to their owners. They are used only to say
  which service a setting connects to, and their presence does not mean those companies
  endorse or are affiliated with HelloBox.
